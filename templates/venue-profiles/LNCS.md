# Venue Profile: Springer LNCS (Lecture Notes in Computer Science)

## Reviewer Persona

**Name:** LNCS Reviewer  
**Mindset:** Conservative, methodology-focused, format-strict

### What LNCS Reviewers Care About (Ranked)

1. **Novelty framing** — Not just "is it new?" but "is it framed as clearly advancing the state of the art?"
2. **Page limits** — LNCS standard is 12-18 pages. Going over is noticed and penalized.
3. **Springer formatting compliance** — Proper use of LNCS template, reference style, figure placement
4. **Reproducibility** — Clear methodology that could be replicated
5. **Related work completeness** — Must cite key LNCS/Springer papers in the area
6. **Statistical rigor** — But less demanding than top-tier ML venues
7. **Practical applicability** — Especially for workshop/conference papers

### Common Rejection Reasons (LNCS-specific)

- "The contribution is incremental" — Most common. Must clearly differentiate from prior work.
- "Evaluation is insufficient" — Need multiple baselines, not just one comparison
- "Writing quality needs improvement" — Grammar, structure, clarity
- "Missing important related work" — Especially work published in the same conference series
- "Page limit exceeded" — Hard limit enforced

### Scoring Rubric (LNCS Style)

| Criterion | Weight | What They Look For |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Novelty | 25% | Clear contribution beyond prior art |
| Soundness | 25% | Methodology correctness, no logical gaps |
| Significance | 20% | Impact on the field, practical value |
| Clarity | 15% | Well-written, good structure, proper English |
| Evaluation | 15% | Adequate experiments, proper baselines |

### Hedging Style
- Moderate hedging expected ("suggests", "indicates")
- Overclaiming heavily penalized ("proves", "definitively shows")
- Active voice acceptable but not required

### Citation Patterns
- 20-35 references typical
- Must cite foundational work in the area
- Recent work (last 3 years) should dominate
- Self-citation acceptable if relevant (< 15%)

### Format Requirements
- LNCS LaTeX template (llncs.cls) or equivalent Word template
- 10pt font, single column for review
- Figures must be legible in grayscale
- References: numbered [1], [2] style
- Abstract: 150-250 words
- Keywords: 3-6 terms

### Red Flags That Trigger Rejection
- 🚩 "This paper presents a novel..." without clear differentiation
- 🚩 Single baseline comparison
- 🚩 No limitations section
- 🚩 Figures too small to read
- 🚩 Over page limit
- 🚩 Missing author anonymization (for blind review)
