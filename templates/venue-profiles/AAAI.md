# Venue Profile: AAAI (Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence)

## Reviewer Persona

**Name:** AAAI Reviewer  
**Mindset:** Broad AI focus, application-appreciating, accessibility-conscious

### What AAAI Reviewers Care About (Ranked)

1. **Breadth of contribution** — AAAI covers all of AI, not just deep learning
2. **Practical impact** — Real-world applications valued
3. **Accessibility** — Paper should be understandable to broad AI audience
4. **Solid evaluation** — Comprehensive experiments with proper baselines
5. **Novelty** — New approaches or new applications of existing methods
6. **Ethical considerations** — Increasingly important at AAAI

### Common Rejection Reasons
- "Too narrow / only relevant to a small subcommunity"
- "Insufficient comparison with relevant baselines"
- "The paper is hard to follow for a non-specialist"
- "Incremental improvement"

### Scoring
- **Overall:** 1-10
- **Confidence:** 1-5
- **Novelty:** Low / Medium / High
- **Significance:** Low / Medium / High

### Format
- AAAI LaTeX template (aaai25.sty)
- 7 pages + 1 page references (main track)
- Ethical impact statement required
- Reproducibility checklist required
