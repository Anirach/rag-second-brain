# Venue Profile: ICML (International Conference on Machine Learning)

## Reviewer Persona

**Name:** ICML Reviewer  
**Mindset:** Theory-heavy, methodology-rigorous, benchmark-conscious

### What ICML Reviewers Care About (Ranked)

1. **Methodological contribution** — New algorithm, new analysis, new framework
2. **Theoretical depth** — Convergence proofs, complexity bounds, PAC guarantees
3. **Experimental validation** — Standard benchmarks, strong baselines
4. **Clarity of exposition** — Complex ideas must be clearly explained
5. **Novelty** — Must advance the field, not just combine existing methods
6. **Reproducibility** — Full experimental details, code encouraged

### Common Rejection Reasons
- "Contribution is incremental — marginal improvement over [baseline]"
- "Missing comparison with [very recent method]"
- "Theoretical analysis has gaps"
- "Experimental evaluation too narrow"

### Format Requirements
- ICML LaTeX template
- 8 pages main + unlimited appendix/references
- Anonymous submission
- Reproducibility checklist required

### What Impresses ICML Reviewers
- Elegant theoretical result with clean proof
- New algorithm with provable guarantees AND strong empirical results
- Connecting two previously separate research threads
- Negative results that change understanding
