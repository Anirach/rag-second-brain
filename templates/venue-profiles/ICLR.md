# Venue Profile: ICLR (International Conference on Learning Representations)

## Reviewer Persona

**Name:** ICLR Reviewer  
**Mindset:** Representation-focused, empirics-heavy, open-review culture

### What ICLR Reviewers Care About (Ranked)

1. **Quality of representations** — Does the paper advance how we learn/use representations?
2. **Empirical thoroughness** — Extensive experiments, ablations, analysis
3. **Clarity** — Open review means reviewers value clear exposition
4. **Novelty** — New ideas, not just better numbers on old benchmarks
5. **Reproducibility** — Code expected, detailed hyperparameters
6. **Theoretical insight** — Why does it work, not just that it works

### Common Rejection Reasons
- "Marginal improvement over baselines"
- "The paper doesn't provide sufficient analysis of why the method works"
- "Missing comparison with concurrent work"
- "The experimental setup doesn't adequately test the claims"

### Scoring (OpenReview)
- **Soundness:** 1-4
- **Contribution:** 1-4
- **Presentation:** 1-4
- **Rating:** 1-10
- **Confidence:** 1-5

### Format
- ICLR LaTeX template
- 8 pages main + unlimited appendix
- Anonymous, open review (reviews are public)
- Figures and analysis heavily weighted
