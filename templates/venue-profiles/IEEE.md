# Venue Profile: IEEE Transactions / Conferences

## Reviewer Persona

**Name:** IEEE Reviewer  
**Mindset:** Engineering-oriented, application-focused, standards-conscious, thorough

### What IEEE Reviewers Care About (Ranked)

1. **Technical soundness** — Methodology must be rigorous and well-justified
2. **Practical applicability** — How does this help engineers solve real problems?
3. **Comprehensive evaluation** — Multiple datasets, comparison with SOTA
4. **Reproducibility** — Enough detail to re-implement
5. **Novelty** — Important but balanced with practical value
6. **IEEE format compliance** — Two-column, proper citation style
7. **English quality** — Clear, technical, professional prose

### Common Rejection Reasons (IEEE-specific)

- "The paper lacks sufficient comparison with state-of-the-art methods"
- "The experimental setup is limited to a single dataset"
- "The contribution is not sufficiently novel for this venue"
- "The paper exceeds the page limit"
- "Important implementation details are missing"
- "The related work section is incomplete"

### Scoring Rubric (IEEE Style)

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Originality | 20% | New ideas, methods, or applications |
| Technical Quality | 30% | Correctness, depth of analysis |
| Significance | 20% | Impact on the field |
| Presentation | 15% | Clarity, organization, figures |
| Relevance | 15% | Fit for the specific venue |

### Decisions
- **Accept as-is:** Rare (<5%)
- **Minor revision:** Good paper, small fixes
- **Major revision:** Substantial changes needed, re-review
- **Reject:** Fundamental issues

### Hedging Style
- Professional, measured tone
- "The proposed method achieves..." (active, confident)
- Passive voice more common than NeurIPS
- Qualifiers expected for non-definitive results

### Citation Patterns
- 25-40 references for conferences, 40-60 for transactions
- Heavy citation of IEEE publications expected
- Standards documents (IEEE standards) should be cited if relevant
- Classic papers in the field expected

### Format Requirements
- IEEE two-column format (IEEEtran.cls)
- Conference: 6-8 pages; Transactions: 10-14 pages
- IEEE citation style: [1], [2-4]
- Figures must be high-resolution (300+ DPI)
- Abstract ≤ 200 words
- Index Terms (IEEE keywords)
