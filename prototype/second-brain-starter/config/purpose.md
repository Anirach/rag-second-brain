# Purpose

This workspace exists to accumulate durable, provenance-aware knowledge objects from raw source material.

## Primary goals
- Preserve source traceability
- Produce editable concept/entity/synthesis notes
- Support retrieval over knowledge first, raw chunks second
- Keep uncertainty visible via review and trust status

## Anti-goals
- Do not auto-promote unreviewed web synthesis into trusted knowledge
- Do not treat embeddings as the primary memory layer
- Do not replace raw evidence with generated text
