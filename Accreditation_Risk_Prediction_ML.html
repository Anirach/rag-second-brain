📉Score Band × Lapse Rate — Predictive Signal

📈 Finding: Hospitals with score_64 ≥ 2.90 have only 19.5% expiry rate vs 35.4% for score_64 2.30–2.49. Lower survey scores are a statistically significant predictor of accreditation lapse (χ² p<0.01). The model assigns 18.2% importance weight to this feature.

🔄Accreditation Cycle Length × Lapse Risk

🔑 Insight: Hospitals on <2-year cycles have 44.4% lapse rate — significantly higher than 2-3 year cycles (12.1%). Short cycles often indicate conditional/probationary accreditation. Renewal count is the 2nd most important ML feature (15.3%).
