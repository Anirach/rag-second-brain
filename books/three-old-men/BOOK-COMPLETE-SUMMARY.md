### Story Summary

**Three Old Men: The Last Conversation** is a literary novel about three elderly friends—Somchai (the Wise One), Prasert (the Skeptic), and Anong (the Storyteller)—who meet weekly at a tea house in Bangkok.

Through seven chapters, they discuss:
- The nature of time and memory
- Love, marriage, and attachment
- The illusion of control over our lives
- The interconnection of joy and suffering
- Regret, forgiveness, and reconciliation
- The meaning of death and mortality

**THE TWIST (Chapter 7):** The reader discovers that Somchai and Anong died years ago. Prasert—the skeptic who never believed in anything beyond the material world—has been coming to the tea house alone for three years, talking to empty chairs, keeping his friends "alive" through imagined conversations. The tea house owner finally tells him the truth.

**TRAGIC ENDING:** Prasert grieves, but eventually reconciles with his estranged son in California. He dreams of his friends one last time, returns to Bangkok, and dies peacefully. The tea house owner reserves their table forever.
